2. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage and the storage used by a mobile app, work in a similar way: they keep small pieces of information on your device so that a website or app can remember something between pages or visits.

In this policy we use the word “cookies” to cover all of these technologies.

3. Our Approach

We keep our use of cookies to a minimum. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell information about your visits or use cookies to build advertising profiles.

We use:

  • Essential cookies, which the Platform needs in order to work. These are always on.
  • Optional cookies, which we only use if you agree. You can change your mind at any time.

4. Essential Cookies

These are needed to provide the Platform and the services you ask for. Because they are strictly necessary, we do not need your consent to use them, but you can block them in your browser or device settings (some parts of the Platform may then not work).

  • Sign-in — keeps you signed in to your account, the business portal or the admin area, and signs you out after a period of inactivity. Kept until you sign out.
  • Your cookie choices (mh_cookie_consent_v1) — remembers whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies, so we don't ask on every page. Kept until you change your choice or clear your browser data.
  • Notifications (mh_push_endpoint, mh_push_feed_seen_at, mh_push_feed_cleared) — if you turn on notifications, these remember your device's notification registration and which notifications you have read or cleared.
  • App settings (mh_permissions_welcome_v1) — remembers that the app's welcome screen has been shown.
  • Map performance (map-pins-v2) — a temporary copy of the business locations shown on our maps, so they load quickly.
  • Security — helps protect the Platform, your account and our systems against misuse.

5. Optional Cookies

We only use these if you choose to allow them.

5.1 Analytics

  • Anonymous visitor ID (mh_view_session) — a random ID stored on your device and sent with page-view counts. It helps us and the businesses listed on the Platform understand how pages, articles, offers and events are viewed, and helps us spot misuse of those statistics. It does not contain your name, email address or other contact details. Kept until you clear your browser data or withdraw your consent, when we delete it.

If you do not allow analytics cookies, we still count page views so that business statistics are accurate, but nothing is stored on your device for this purpose.

5.2 Features From Other Providers

Some features are provided by other companies, which may set their own cookies when the feature loads. We do not control these cookies; please see each provider's own cookie or privacy policy.

  • Chat assistant (Elfsight) — the chat assistant on our website. It only loads if you allow features from other providers.
  • Interactive maps (OpenStreetMap) — the embedded maps on our Getting Here, Transport and Parking pages. They only load if you allow features from other providers, or if you choose “Show map” for a particular map.

6. Other Services We Use

To show maps, weather, fonts and address look-ups, the Platform requests information from other services (for example OpenFreeMap map tiles, Google Fonts, Open-Meteo and postcodes.io). These requests share technical information such as your IP address so that the content can be delivered, but these services do not set cookies through our Platform.

Payments for business subscriptions are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own secure pages, which use their own cookies under Stripe's policies.

Links to other websites, including social media and businesses' own websites, take you to sites with their own cookie policies.

7. Managing Your Choices

When you first visit Maidenhead.com you can accept all optional cookies, reject them, or choose which to allow. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link at the bottom of every page on the website, or under Explore → Cookie Settings in The Maidenhead App.

The Maidenhead App does not use optional cookies unless you turn them on in Cookie Settings.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser or device settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the Platform, such as signing in, from working.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or services. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date it was last updated. Where a change affects optional cookies, we will ask for your choice again.
